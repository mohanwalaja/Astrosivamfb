## 4. Ordering, delivery, payment and refunds

### Ordering and account features

- Sign in with **Google or email**; the Birth page offers Google sign-in to use
  saved profiles or family members and track orders.
- Optional saved birth profiles/family members are saved only when the user
  selects the relevant save option.
- Birth Jathagam cart copy says **up to 6 orders in cart** and **place next order
  after payment**.
- The public How It Works journey is Sign in → Service → Details → Payment →
  Analysis → Delivery.
- Place search supplies coordinates; exact date/time and ceremony/month
  preferences are collected as applicable.

### Delivery

- Reports are reviewed on a **six-hour cadence**. This is not a promise that every
  order is delivered within six hours.
- After priest-team verification and administrator approval, the personalised
  **PDF report and official tax invoice** are sent together to the registered email.
- Order history and live delivery status are maintained in the **Customer Dashboard**.
- Report/certificate languages remain **English, Tamil and Hindi**.
- The homepage states that every paid report includes **seven days of personal
  astrologer chat** after delivery.
- Sample PDFs use fixed example details, not customer data.

### Payments

- India: **Google Pay / UPI** direct transfer to the verified registered account.
- Worldwide: **PayPal**, including PayPal balance and major cards.
- Cards listed: **VISA, Mastercard, American Express and Discover**; the payment
  policy says a PayPal account is not required for card checkout.
- Paid orders require the transaction reference/receipt number and administrator
  review before calculations are finalised and dispatched.

### Refund and cancellation

- Before administrator approval: **90% refund**; the retained 10% covers
  non-returned processing charges.
- Refund settlement: **3 working days**, to the original payment method and
  currency; a provider may take one further working day to display the credit.
- After approval and email delivery of the personalised report/invoice: completed
  and non-refundable.
- Wrong birth details corrected before report generation: **no correction charge**.
- Free Beta orders involve no payment and therefore no refund; they may be
  cancelled before approval.
- Verified duplicate or orphaned payments: **100% returned**.
- Cancellation requests use the registered email and the `ASTRO-` order number.

## 5. Privacy and consent changes relevant to publishing

The October 2026 Privacy Policy now explicitly states:

- Essential sign-in, security and checkout storage remains active; optional
  first-party visitor statistics, **Google Analytics 4** and **Meta Pixel** run
  only after **Accept all**. **Reject all** keeps essential features working.
- An incomplete report form can remain for up to **30 minutes in this browser
  tab's session storage**. It is not a device-wide saved profile and disappears
  when the tab closes or the draft is used/cleared.
- Birth names, dates/times, birthplaces, coordinates and report contents are not
  included in analytics or advertising events.
- Signed-in age-band measurement requires the relevant adult's explicit consent;
  only the saved birth date is used server-side, never birth time. Exact age,
  birth date and account ID are not put into analytics events/tables.
- First-party analytics identifiers are retained for up to **400 days** and shown
  to administrators as aggregate reports. Raw IP addresses, account IDs, full
  referrers, query strings and user-agent strings are not stored in those tables.
- Google Analytics page measurement strips the query string; Meta PageView is
  skipped when the URL contains a query or fragment.
- Access, correction or deletion requests go to `admin@astrosivam.com`, subject to
  service, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention and legal retention requirements.

The Messenger FAQ and Meta compliance library now point to these rules. This scan
does not replace the full policy at <https://astrosivam.com/privacy-policy>.
