**How the auto-select works (you can try on the live form):**

1. Open https://astrosivam.com/birth-jathagam
2. Fill **Date of Birth · Exact Birth Time · Birth Place** (plus name/gender).
3. The plan strip above the form recomputes `ageYears = floor((today − DOB)/365.2425)` client-side and highlights:
   - `0–10 → Kids Jadagam`
   - `11–18 → Students Jadagam`
